Project Overview

This project uses a reproducible retail sales dataset to analyze business performance across product categories, regions, revenue distributions, and numeric relationships. The Python pipeline exports the dataset, PNG charts, and written report files.

Methodology and Data Preparation

This section adds the formal report structure commonly expected in an analytical submission: analytical statement, dataset structure, preparation checks, and validation outputs.

1. Define Evaluate retail sales performance by time, category, region, distribution, and numeric relationships.
2. Prepare Generate reproducible order-level sales data, validate missing values, and create business metrics.
3. Analyze Use aggregations, distribution plots, outlier rules, Pearson correlations, and pairwise visual checks.
4. Report Export PNG charts, CSV summary tables, and written interpretation files for submission.

Sales Trend Summary

Line charts show daily and monthly sales movement, while bar and pie charts compare category performance and share. Electronics is the leading category with $2,231,105.24 in revenue, and March is the best revenue month.

  • Total records: 5,599
  • Total revenue: $4,245,014.25

Distribution Interpretation

Revenue is right-skewed, meaning most orders are medium-value while a smaller group of premium orders creates a longer upper tail. The IQR method identifies 412 high-revenue outliers above $1,992.17.

  • Skewness: 2.01
  • Highest median region: North
  • Lowest median region: South

Correlation Summary

The strongest positive relationship is between profit and revenue at 0.97. The strongest negative relationship is between profit margin and marketing spend at -0.31.

  • Heatmap uses Pearson correlation
  • Upper triangle is masked
  • Pairplot compares key sales variables
